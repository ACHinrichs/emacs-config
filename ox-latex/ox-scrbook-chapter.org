#+Title: ox-scrbook-chapter

The scrbook class is used by i5 as a base for thesis templates.
However, in the i5 thesis template, the top most level heading is chapter

#+begin_src emacs-lisp :export code
    (with-eval-after-load 'ox-latex
      (add-to-list 'org-latex-classes
      			   '("scrbook-chapter"
      				 "\\documentclass[a4paper,BCOR5mm,11pt,pointlessnumbers,twoside,openany]{scrbook}
  [NO-DEFAULT-PACKAGES]
  [EXTRA]"
      		   ("\\chapter{%s}". "\\chapter*{%s}")
      		   ("\\section{%s}" . "\\section*{%s}")
      		   ("\\subsection{%s}" . "\\subsection*{%s}")
      		   ("\\subsubsection{%s}" . "\\subsubsection*{%s}")
      		   ("\\paragraph{%s}" . "\\paragraph*{%s}")
      		   ("\\subparagraph{%s}" . "\\subparagraph*{%s}"))))
#+end_src
